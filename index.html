What I check

Every visit includes

The same careful checklist every season, so small problems get caught while they're still small.

HVAC filter replacement

For every covered system, so the upstairs furnace filter doesn't get forgotten.

Leak-sensor check and wet test

Status and batteries on every sensor, plus a physical wet test on the water heater and sump sensors.

Sump-pump check

Water goes in the pit and the pump has to run, so a failed float shows up before the storm that matters.

Smoke and CO alarm check

Every alarm tested, and any that are past their replacement date get written down.

The seasonal plan

Four visits, one year, built for Central Ohio

Central Ohio houses get hard freezes, spring storms, and humid summers. Each visit covers the full checklist plus the task that season calls for, along with a few quick looks while I'm there.

Visit one

Winter

Seasonal task: a freeze-risk walk through the house.

  • Pipes in cold spots checked: garage walls, crawlspaces, rim joists, sinks on outside walls
  • Outside faucet covers still on, lines still drained
  • Whole-house humidifier pad checked, if you have one
  • Attic hatch look for frost or signs of ice dams
  • Sump discharge outlet clear of ice and snow
Visit two

Spring

Seasonal task: getting ready for storm season.

  • Sump pump, check valve, and discharge line checked before the heavy rains
  • Downspout extensions in place and pointing away from the foundation
  • Outside faucets turned back on and checked for freeze damage
  • Window wells and grading near the foundation looked over
  • AC condensate drain checked before cooling season
Visit three

Summer

Seasonal task: basement humidity and AC drainage.

  • AC condensate line flushed, with the drain pan and condensate pump checked
  • Basement humidity read, plus the dehumidifier filter and drain if you have one
  • Water heater drain pan and surrounding area looked over
  • Dryer vent flap checked outside for lint buildup
  • Caulk around tubs, showers, and outside wall penetrations looked over
Visit four

Fall

Seasonal task: freeze prep before the first hard freeze.

  • Hoses off, outside faucets drained, and inside shutoffs closed where you have them
  • Insulated cover on each outside faucet
  • Gutter outlets and downspouts checked for leaf clogs
  • Sump discharge line routed so it won't freeze or pool by the foundation
  • A note if your irrigation system still needs its blowout (that's a separate trade)

The Home Record

Everything about your house, on one page

Model numbers, filter sizes, how old each unit is, and where the shutoffs are. Every visit adds dated photos and notes, so the next decision about your house starts from facts.

  • Dated photos of what was checked and what was found
  • Equipment list with ages, model numbers, and filter sizes
  • Where the main water shutoff is, and whether it turns
  • Notes on anything worth watching, or worth calling a licensed trade about

FAQ

Common questions

How much does the membership cost?

The Founding Membership is from $699 for year one and is limited to the first 25 homes. Regular base membership is from $799/year, and setup is from $199. Base pricing covers homes with up to 3 bathrooms, 1 HVAC system, and 1 water heater, and half baths count. Final pricing comes after Justin reviews your home.

How fast will I get a quote?

The form takes about 2 minutes. Justin reviews your home and sends your quote within one business day.

Do you do repairs?

No. This is a maintenance service. If I find something that needs a licensed plumber, electrician, or HVAC tech, I'll tell you and you choose who to call. Old or stuck valves get tagged and noted, not forced.

What's in the Home Record?

A dated record of every visit, with photos, notes on what was done and what was found, and your equipment details like model numbers, filter sizes, unit ages, and where the shutoffs are.

Do I need Wi-Fi for the leak sensors?

Yes. The leak-sensor hub needs internet access, either Wi-Fi or an open router connection in the home.

How does the Fall Freeze Prep credit work?

The Fall Freeze Prep visit is $129. If you join the membership within 30 days of that visit, the $129 comes off your first year. More about Fall Freeze Prep.

My home is on a well. Can you still help?

Yes. Homes on a well and pressure tank get a custom look before I quote, because that equipment changes what each visit should cover.

Do I need to be home for visits?

Visits are scheduled ahead at a time that works for you. Most homeowners are home for the first walkthrough. After that, we can work out access that's comfortable for you.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled securely through Square. Your quote spells out the payment schedule.

Can I cancel?

Your membership terms are spelled out in your agreement before you sign up, and if your plans change, just talk to Justin.
