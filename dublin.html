What I see in Dublin homes

Every house is different, but the same few things come up again and again in Dublin.

  • Two of everything.A second furnace means a second filter, and the upstairs one is the one that gets forgotten. The membership price includes one HVAC system and one water heater, with a flat yearly adder for each extra, so every filter in the house is on the same schedule.
  • Water heaters on a finished level.When the mechanical room sits beside a finished basement, a leaking tank ruins carpet and drywall before anyone notices. That is the first place a leak sensor goes.
  • Many outside faucets, and an irrigation system.Big lots mean four or five spigots. All of them get the hose off, drained, and covered before the first hard freeze. Irrigation blowouts are a separate trade, and I will tell you if yours still needs one.
  • Equipment records that live in a drawer.Model numbers, filter sizes, the age of each unit, where the shutoffs are. The Home Record puts all of it on one page so the next decision about the house starts from facts.
