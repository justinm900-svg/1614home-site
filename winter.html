For homes anywhere in Columbus, from Hilliard and Dublin to Plain City, Upper Arlington, and everywhere in between. I cover the same items as Fall Freeze Prep, shifted to the middle of winter, and you get a written Home Record when I leave.

What the visit covers

  • Outdoor faucets and hose bibs Covers still on, hoses still off, and a look for drips or splits from the cold. The inside shutoff stays closed where there is one.
  • Furnace filter changed Winter air loads a filter faster. Use your own filter, or I bring the right size and charge only what it costs me.
  • Sump pump test Water in the pit, float raised, pump runs and drains. I look at the discharge outlet so ice and snow are not blocking it.
  • Smoke and CO alarms tested Test button on every alarm, with a note on any that are past their replacement date.
  • Main water shutoff found and tagged So you, or anyone in the house, can find it fast. Old or stuck valves are tagged, not forced.
  • A written Home Record afterward What I did and anything worth watching, written down before I leave.

Common questions

Will I get a report on the house?
You get a written Home Record of what I did and anything worth watching. It is a maintenance visit, not a report on the condition of the house. Nothing is opened up, and nothing is graded.
What if my outside faucet has no inside shutoff?
Many newer homes in Hilliard, Dublin, and Plain City have frost free faucets with no separate valve. In winter I make sure the hose is off and a cover is on. A standard faucet with no shutoff gets a cover and a note, since adding a shutoff is a plumber's job.
Do I need to supply a furnace filter?
Either way works. Text me the size on the edge of your current filter and I will bring one and charge only what it costs me, or leave yours by the furnace.
How long are you booking Winter Visits?
I'm booking Winter Visits from December 1 through the end of February.
How do I pay?
Card at the end of the visit through Square, or a Square invoice by text or email. $129 flat, plus the filter at cost if I supply it.
