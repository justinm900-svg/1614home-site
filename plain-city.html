What I see in Plain City homes

Every house is different, but the same few things come up again and again in Plain City.

  • New construction with settling to watch.In a house under ten years old, the equipment is new but the first few years turn up loose things: a sump discharge that never got buried properly, a water heater pan with no drain, alarms installed on the builder's schedule rather than the right one. The first visit catches the easy ones.
  • Frost free faucets and builder grade covers.Newer homes almost always have frost free spigots. The hose comes off and a cover goes on, and that is most of the winterizing. The inside shutoff, if there is one, gets tagged.
  • Older village houses with their original plumbing layout.A hundred year old house in the village may have a shutoff in a crawlspace or a cellar, and an outside faucet that needs the inside valve closed and drained. I find the valve, make sure it moves, and tag it.
  • Wells and septic on the edges of town.Some properties outside the village are on a well and pressure tank. Those get a custom look before I quote, because a pressure tank and a well pump change what the visit should cover.
