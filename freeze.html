Common questions

Will I get a report on the house?
You get a short written note of what I did and anything worth watching. It is a maintenance visit, not a report on the condition of the house. Nothing is opened up, and nothing is graded.
What if my outside faucet has no inside shutoff?
Many newer homes in Hilliard, Dublin, and Plain City have frost free faucets with no separate valve. For those, the hose comes off, the line drains, and a cover goes on. A standard faucet with no shutoff gets a cover and a note, since adding a shutoff is a plumber's job.
Do I need to supply a furnace filter?
Either way works. Text me the size on the edge of your current filter and I will bring one and charge only what it costs me, or leave yours by the furnace.
When is the first hard freeze in central Ohio?
Most years the Columbus area sees its first night at or below 28 degrees in late October or early November. Visits start Monday, November 2, and go through the end of November.
How do I pay?
Card at the end of the visit through Square, or a Square invoice by text or email. $129 flat, plus the filter at cost if I supply it.
