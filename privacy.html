Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

1614 Home LLC, doing business as 1614 Home Co. ("we," "us"), runs 1614home.co. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you request a quote or contact us: your name, phone number, email, home address, and details about your home such as bathrooms, HVAC systems, water heaters, and sump pump.
  • Text message consent, if you choose to give it.
  • Basic website usage data collected by Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, approximate location (city), device type, and how you found our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote, schedule service, and keep in touch about your membership.
  • To understand how people find and use our website so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run the business: Jotform (quote forms and agreements), Google (email and website analytics), and Square (payments). They handle your information only to provide those services. We may also share information when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics places cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out tool at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Text messages

Text consent is optional and not required to request a quote. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us. Members' Home Records are kept as described in the Membership Agreement.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
